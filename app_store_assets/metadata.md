Lock Kiya Jaye is India’s premier sports turf and venue booking platform. Whether you are organizing a weekend football clash with friends, a late-night box cricket tournament, or an intense badminton session, Lock Kiya Jaye makes discovering and reserving sports grounds effortless.

Say goodbye to endless phone calls and double-booked grounds. Find top-rated turfs nearby, check live slot availability in real time, and lock your match in just a few taps!

Never get double-booked. View live morning, evening, and prime night match schedules with up-to-the-minute updates.
